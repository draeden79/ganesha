BEFORE YOUR FIRST STEP

Every good journey
starts with
a question.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You will explore AI tools using natural language: describe what you need, provide context and learn to improve the result. Your curiosity is the starting point.

Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The journey starts with the fundamentals and moves on to prompts, creative work and everyday applications. You build understanding before tackling more involved tasks.

How can I use AI in everyday life?

You can use it to explore ideas, create drafts, organize information and plan activities. Throughout the journey, the exercises connect to real challenges in your routine.

Does AI replace my perspective and creativity?

AI offers possibilities; you set the intention, make choices and evaluate the result. Your context, critical thinking and creativity remain essential at every step.
